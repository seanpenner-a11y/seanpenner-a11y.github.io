Wallpaper Installation — Franklin, Brentwood & Nashville, TN

The lost art, hung by hand.

Skilled wallpaper installers are nearly extinct. I hang grasscloth, mural panels, hand-printed and wide-width papers across Williamson County and greater Nashville — and I prepare the wall underneath, so the paper goes on flat and stays that way.

Talk to Sean

Why it matters

The wall is half the job

Most wallpaper failures aren't the paper — they're the wall. I'm a finish carpenter and remodeler by trade, which means I skim-coat, repair, and true the wall before a single drop is cut. Flat walls, invisible seams, patterns matched to the millimeter.

What I hang

Every paper, treated like it's irreplaceable

  • Grasscloth & natural fibers — no seam paste-bleed, no crushed texture.
  • Mural & panel papers — sequenced, leveled, and optionally framed in custom molding.
  • Hand-printed & block-printed papers — inspected roll by roll before the first cut.
  • Wide-width & commercial vinyls — clean wraps, tight inside corners.

For Interior Designers

Your spec, hung the way it deserves

I work from your drawings and specifications, receive and inspect client-purchased paper, flag quantity or dye-lot issues before install day, and keep you in the loop at every step. Your client sees drop cloths, clean lines, and a finished wall — you get photos for your portfolio.

Serving design firms across Franklin, Brentwood, and Nashville. One project at a time, scheduled in writing.

Common questions

Wallpaper installation, answered straight

Do you install wallpaper in Nashville, or just Franklin?

Both. Home base is Williamson County — Franklin, Brentwood, Spring Hill, Thompson's Station, Nolensville, Leiper's Fork — and I take wallpaper projects across greater Nashville.

Can you fix damaged walls before hanging?

Yes — that's the advantage of hiring a remodeler who hangs paper. Skim coating, drywall repair, and removing old wallpaper are all part of the service.

Do you hang paper the client or designer purchased?

Gladly. I'll tell you honestly how many rolls the room needs before you order, and inspect every roll on arrival.

What does wallpaper installation cost?

It depends on the paper, the pattern repeat, and the condition of your walls. Send photos of the room and the paper you're considering, and you'll get an honest number — fixed price or time-and-materials, whichever saves you money.
