Local, for real

Work done by a neighbor, not a crew

The Franklin Craftsman is based in Spring Hill — this is home. When you call about a wallpaper project or a wainscoting job, you're not waiting on a crew to make the drive down from Nashville; the person who answers the phone is a few minutes away and does the work himself.

Spring Hill is growing fast, and a lot of the new construction here left builder-grade walls waiting for character. That's exactly the work I love: a papered powder bath, a board-and-batten entry, a dining room with picture-frame molding. One project at a time, walls prepared properly, everything caulked, primed, and painted before I call it done.
