What We Do

Where quality matters

We're not the everything contractor — on purpose. We take the projects where craftsmanship shows, and where it lasts.

Bathroom remodels

Premium baths built from the studs out. Waterproofing is never optional, and the work behind the tile gets the same care as the tile itself.

Fine wallpaper

Fine Wallpaper

The lost art, hung by hand.

Here's a secret the industry won't tell you: skilled paperhangers are nearly extinct. Most remodelers won't touch fine paper. Most hangers can't repair the wall underneath it.

We do both — flat walls, invisible seams, patterns matched to the millimeter. Careful with every roll, because at this level there are no practice pieces.

For interior designers

Glad to work from your spec, receive and inspect client-purchased paper, and keep you in the loop at every step. Your client, your design — hung the way it deserves.
